The problem

Food waste is invisible until you measure it.

290M

tonnes of food wasted by the food-service sector worldwide in 2022.

Source: UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024
54%

of Algeria's household and similar waste is food.

Source: Agence nationale des déchets (2023)
100M+

baguettes thrown away in Algeria during Ramadan alone.

Source: Ministry of Commerce, March 2024

And since Law 25-02 (2025), waste in Algeria is treated as a resource to recover. You can't recover what you don't measure.

How it works

Three steps, every time something goes in the bin.

1. Weigh

A scale built into the bin detects each disposal and its weight.

2. Recognise

A camera and AI identify the food, and flag items that don't belong, like plastic or glass.

3. Report

Kitchens see what they waste, how much, and when, in a simple dashboard.

The result

Data that helps kitchens buy smarter, cook the right amounts and portion better.

  • Lower food costsStop paying for food that ends up in the bin.
  • Smarter purchasingKnow which ingredients spoil and which dishes come back.
  • A smaller footprintLess waste, and numbers you can show your customers.

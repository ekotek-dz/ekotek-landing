Privacy notice

We keep this simple: we only collect what you choose to send us, and we only use it to reply to you.

Who is responsible

EKOTEK, an early-stage food-waste startup based in Béjaïa, Algeria. Contact for anything about your data: ekotekdz@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Demo request form: your name, establishment name and type, city, email, phone (optional), meals served per day (optional) and your message.
  • Contact form: your message, and your name and email if you choose to give them.
  • Technical data: the page language and the page you sent the form from. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Your browser only remembers your language choice (EN/FR/عربي/ⵣ) on your own device.

Why we use it

  • To answer your message, organise a demo or a pilot, or send you the ways to donate if you asked.
  • To understand what people expect from EKOTEK (for example, feature ideas).

We use your details only with your consent, which you give by ticking the box before sending a form. We will not send you newsletters or marketing, and we never sell or share your details with other companies for their own use.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored in EKOTEK's Google account (Google Sheets and Gmail). The website is hosted by Vercel. These providers may process data on servers outside Algeria, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months after our last exchange with you, then we delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your rights

Under Algerian Law 18-07 on the protection of personal data, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and you can object to its use. Just email ekotekdz@gmail.com. You can also contact the national authority, the ANPDP (Autorité nationale de protection des données à caractère personnel).
